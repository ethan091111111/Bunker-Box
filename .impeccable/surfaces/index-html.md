# Surface: waiting list (index.html)

Mode: Persuade

## Direction contract

THESIS: A ribboned box waiting in a links bunker at first light, with the waiting list as its packing slip. Joining is putting your name on a box. Built on the team's own logo and colours (olive, cream, sage) and their line "A little mystery, a lot of golf".

OWN-WORLD: A Scottish links course at dawn: raked bunker sand, marram grass, haar, a flag on the green. Olive bands carry the supplied logo; the page is a warm cream ramp with olive-ink text and olive as the one accent. Instrument Serif for headlines, Instrument Sans for the interface, both self-hosted. The Higgsfield-generated hero scene is saved in `images/`. Signature: a flat olive ball marker with a cream "?", used only on the surprise line and for the queue number.

STORY: A golfer or gift buyer sees the box in the bunker, reads that mystery boxes go on sale before Christmas 2026, sees that at least one thing inside is a surprise, and fills in the packing slip. Their name appears on the slip with their queue number in the ball marker.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Olive band with the logo and a "Join the waiting list" link. The headline "Put your name on a box" on the calm sky of the hero scene, with the packing-slip signup card rising from the bottom of the photo. On a 1440x900 desktop the whole slip, submit button included, is visible without scrolling; on phones the headline, subline and photo come first and a dock button leads back to the slip.

FORM: Redesigned with the unslop skill (by Damir Sokolovsky, @heydamir, CC BY 4.0), replacing the earlier tournament leaderboard.

FINISH: DESIGN.md describes the system, and every shipping raster (hero, favicon, touch icon, share image) carries its provenance there.
